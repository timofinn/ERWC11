Purpose

It loses anyway.

Today you figure out how a court can hear the truth and still say no.

I. Two Traps

7 min · Left page, top

A closed loop is an argument with no exit. Every door leads back inside. Copy these two:

The trapHow it works
The CircleThe proof depends on the thing you’re trying to prove. Any evidence counts as more proof.
The Either/OrOnly two choices allowed. You’re with us or against us. Nothing in between.
Mr. Finn’s modelThe Circle: “The group chat decided Jay is fake. Proof? He’s acting weird. Why? Because he knows we know.”
The Either/Or: “Repost it or you’re part of the problem.”

Under the table, write one example of each from real life. Keep it school-appropriate.

II. Watch

20 min · Just watch

Watch the court scenes, from Mary Warren’s confession to Hale walking out. Read Scene 1 first. It catches you up on what happens before.

While you watch: every time someone tries to escape the trap, notice how the court shuts the door.

Scene 1 · Three shots at the court. Giles Corey says Thomas Putnam got his daughter to accuse neighbors so he could grab their land. Giles won’t name his source, so he’s arrested. Francis Nurse hands over a petition: 91 neighbors swear Rebecca, Martha, and Elizabeth are good people. Judge Danforth’s response? Bring all 91 in for questioning. Then Proctor brings in Mary Warren, who signs a paper saying the girls are faking.

Scene 2 · Faint on command. The judges tell Mary to prove she faked it by fainting right there. Without the other girls screaming around her, she can’t. So the court decides her confession is the lie. Proctor goes nuclear: he admits the affair with Abigail, to prove she wants Elizabeth dead. He says his wife never lies. So Danforth calls Elizabeth in, with John’s back turned. Asked why she fired Abigail, Elizabeth lies for the first time in her life to protect John’s name. That lie sinks him.

Scene 3 · The yellow bird. Abigail screams at the ceiling. She says Mary’s spirit is attacking her as a yellow bird. The girls copy every word Mary says until Mary snaps. She flips and accuses Proctor of working for the Devil. Proctor loses it and says the court has killed God. Danforth sends him and Giles to jail. Hale quits the court and slams the door.

IV. Steelman the Judge

8 min · Right page, bottom · Partners

Easy take: Danforth is evil. Harder take: Danforth thinks he’s the only adult in the room.

  1. Partner A steelmans Danforth out loud. One minute. Partner B, initial it if Danforth would sign it.
  2. Swap. Partner B steelmans Hale walking out.
  3. Write your one sentence with the frame:
If I were Danforth, I’d say , because if I’m wrong, .
Last 8 minutes: buffer. Finish your table and get your initials.

A trap doesn’t need a villain. Just a rule nobody’s allowed to question.
