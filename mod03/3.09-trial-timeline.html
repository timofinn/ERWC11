3.00 | A Power Play: The Crucible

3.09 | Trial Timeline

Who talks when, and what you're there to do

◆ ◆ ◆

One trial, seven moves, about seven minutes.

Find your number. Know your job.

When it's your turn, perform it. Don't just read it.

3.09 Performance Score: out of 10, Summative.

I. The Order

  1. 1

    Judge

    30 sec

    Mr. Finn reads the charges. Court is in session.

  2. 2

    Prosecutor 1 · Opening

    40 to 80 sec

    Your best case that the charges are fair. Use what the character did and said. End on a punchy line.

  3. 3

    Defender 1 · Opening

    40 to 80 sec

    Same job, other side. Show their words and actions in a fairer light. End on a punchy line.

  4. 4

    The Character · Clear your name

    40 to 80 sec

    Why you've been misunderstood. Who's really to blame. Then your plea: not guilty.

    Nobody playing the character? Skip this step. See the backup plan below.

  5. 5

    Prosecutor 2 · Closing

    40 to 80 sec

    A different reason they're guilty. Prosecutor 1 already used theirs.

  6. 6

    Defender 2 · Final word

    40 to 80 sec

    The last voice the jury hears. Make it the one they remember.

  7. 7

    Jury

    1 min

    Mr. Finn calls the vote. Guilty or not guilty, then write why on your Jury Sheet.

II. Say It Like an Actor

  • 40 to 80 seconds. Under 40 is a shrug. Over 80 and you've lost them.
  • How you say it counts. Eyes up, slow down, let it land.
  • Pause before your punch line. Silence is a power move.

III. No Character? Backup Plan

  • Skip step 4. Everything else runs the same.
  • Attorney 1 on each side opens. Attorney 2 on each side rebuts.
  • Rebuttal: save your most impactful line for last. That's what the jury walks out with.
