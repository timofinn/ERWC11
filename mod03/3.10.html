Purpose

Court is in session.

You speak once. You listen the rest of the time.

The jury decides the verdict. Your grade doesn’t care who wins.

Summative · Live turn + jury sheet · 15 of your 30 points

I. Courtroom Rules

Read once · Before Trial 1

  • Notes are allowed. Eyes up at least twice.
  • Witnesses stay in character, even on cross.
  • Cross is one question. Not a speech.
  • Attack the argument, not the person.
  • The jury is silent and writing.

II. Order of Events

22 min per trial · The judge keeps time

  1. The judge reads the charge. 30 sec
  2. Prosecution opening, then defense opening. 90 sec each
  3. Three prosecution witnesses. One cross question each. 2 min each
  4. Three defense witnesses. One cross question each. 2 min each
  5. Defense closing, then prosecution closing. 90 sec each
The timer is the law. When it hits zero, finish your sentence and sit down.

III. Jury Duty

Every trial you’re not in · Jury sheet

  1. Catch one steelman. Who said it, and what was it?
  2. Catch one trap. Circle or Either/Or?
  3. At the verdict, stand up and walk. Responsible to the windows. Not Responsible to the door.

A trap doesn’t need a villain. Just a rule nobody’s allowed to question.

Success Looks Like

  • Said it: loud enough, inside the time, eyes up twice 5 pts
  • Used it: evidence, power, or rhetoric from your prep card, out loud 5 pts
  • Jury sheet: one steelman, one trap, your verdict 5 pts
