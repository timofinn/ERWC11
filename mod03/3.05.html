3.00 · A Power Play

3.05 | Who Holds the Power?

❖ ❖ ❖

Purpose

Act One is a town deciding who gets to be scary.

Today you pick one character and name where their power comes from.

Then you prove it twice: on paper, then out loud.

Left page5 forms of power + the frame
Right pageYour paragraph

I. Five Kinds of Power

5 min · Left page, top

Copy the form and the tell. One line each.

FormThe tell
LegitimateA title or a job. “I’m in charge here.”
RewardHanding out good stuff. “Help me and you win.”
ExpertKnowing more. “Trust me, I’ve read the books.”
ReferentBeing admired. “People want to be on my side.”
CoerciveThreats. “Do it or else.”

Most characters use more than one. Pick the one they lean on.

II. Watch One

5 min · Left page, bottom

Mr. Finn models Parris on the board. Copy the frame under your five forms.

Claim: uses power in Act One.
Proof 1: “” (p. ). This shows because .
Proof 2: Same shape. New quote.

III. Write It

15 min · Right page

  1. Pick one: Abigail, Putnam, Proctor, Hale, or Rebecca Nurse. Parris is taken.
  2. Find two Act One moments where they use power. Mark the page numbers.
  3. Write one paragraph, 5 to 7 sentences, using the frame.
  4. Quotes go in quotation marks. Page number goes in parentheses.
Stuck on a quote? Flip through your Act One annotations first, then ask your table.

IV. Say It

15 min · Two rounds, two partners

  1. Partner A talks for 60 seconds: claim, two proofs, the “because.” Notes are fine. Reading it word for word is not.
  2. Partner B checks Claim, Proof, and Why on A’s page, then initials.
  3. B gives one glow and one grow. 30 seconds.
  4. Switch. Then repeat with a new partner, 45 seconds this time.
  5. Last 2 minutes: bottom of your right page, one sentence. Which form of power was hardest to prove, and why?
Glow: “Your was convincing because .”
Grow: “Next time, add to your .”

Mr. Finn is circulating with the stamp while you talk.

Success Looks Like

  • Claim: one character, one form of power 4 pts
  • Proof: two quotes, each with a page number 4 pts
  • Why: you explain how each quote shows the power 4 pts

4 = nailed it. 2 = partly there. 0 = missing. Say it twice with your partner’s initials to earn the stamp.
