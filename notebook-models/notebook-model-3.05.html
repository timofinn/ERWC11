Box 3 · My Paragraph (5 to 7 sentences)
Parris uses legitimate power in Act One. When he says "my ministry's at stake" (p. 11), he shows that his authority comes from his job. This shows legitimate power because he panics when the title is in danger. He also says "a minister is the Lord's man in the parish" (p.    ), which treats crossing him as crossing God. This shows legitimate power because he uses the title as his reason to be obeyed. He backs it up with a threat about the church burning, so coercive power is in the room too.
